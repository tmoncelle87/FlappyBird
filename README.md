# Pipe Rampage

A 2D side-scrolling arcade game inspired by the classic Flappy Bird. Take to the skies, navigate your character through an endless gauntlet of green pipes, and see how high you can push your score!

## Features
* **Classic Arcade Gameplay:** Dodge obstacles and time your jumps perfectly to survive as long as possible.
* **High Score Tracking:** Compete against yourself! The game actively tracks your current score and automatically saves your all-time high score across play sessions.
* **Character Selection:** Swap out your player avatar in the character menu. Choose your favorite flyer from a roster that includes a classic bird, a chicken, and a pig.
* **Save System:** Lock in your character preferences with a built-in save feature so your favorite avatar is always ready.
* **Audio Controls:** Easily adjust the game's music volume using the interactive slider in the Options menu.
* **Clean UI:** Fully functional main menu, options, and character selection screens featuring a cohesive mountain-range aesthetic.

## Controls
* **Jump/Flap:** Spacebar 
* **Navigate Menus:** Mouse click

## Installation & Setup
1. Clone the repository to your local machine.
2. Open the project in your preferred game engine or IDE.
3. Build and run the project to start playing!
